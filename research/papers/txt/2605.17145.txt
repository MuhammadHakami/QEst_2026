Fig. 1. Overview of our heuristic-assisted differentiable bilevel optimization on a 12-variable problem, which is encoded into 4 qubits. The quantum circuit
produces PCE correlators that decode to a soft commitment schedule ỹ. A classical QP computes the dispatch p∗ (and dual variables), which defines the
training loss. Gradients are obtained by combining QP dual sensitivities with parameter-shift derivatives, and circuit parameters are updated with the Adam
optimizer. After training, a threshold sweep produces a hard schedule and a final dispatch.
